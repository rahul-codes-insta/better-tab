Download the engine

A depth model (Depth Anything V2 Small) that works out what’s near and far in your photo. About 27 MB, downloaded once. You can remove it any time in Settings.

Tips for great results

Pick a landscape photo with open sky or space in the top half and a clear shape rising into it, like mountains, a skyline, dunes or a lone tree.

Use a large image (2560 px wide or more). After the engine finds the depth, drag Clock depth until the clock tucks behind the scene, and use the brush to fix any edges.
